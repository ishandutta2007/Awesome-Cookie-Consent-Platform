# Resources: Further Reading & Tools for Digital Twin Biomimicry Specialists

## Landmark Papers & Texts

### Digital Twins
- Grieves & Vickers (2017) — "Digital Twin: Mitigating Unpredictable, Undesirable Emergent Behavior in Complex Systems" — foundational digital twin concept paper
- Tao et al. (2019) — "Digital Twins and Cyber–Physical Systems toward Smart Manufacturing and Industry 4.0" (Engineering)

### Biomechanics & FSI
- Fung (1993) — "Biomechanics: Mechanical Properties of Living Tissues" — foundational biomechanics textbook
- Holzapfel, Gasser & Ogden (2000) — "A New Constitutive Framework for Arterial Wall Mechanics" — HGO model foundational paper
- Peskin (2002) — "The immersed boundary method" (Acta Numerica) — foundational FSI method paper

### Topology Optimization
- Bendsøe & Sigmund (2004) — "Topology Optimization: Theory, Methods and Applications" — standard topology optimization reference
- Fratzl & Weinkamer (2007) — "Nature's hierarchical materials" (Progress in Materials Science) — bio-inspired materials review

### Data Assimilation
- Evensen (2009) — "Data Assimilation: The Ensemble Kalman Filter" — EnKF reference text
- Reich & Cotter (2015) — "Probabilistic Forecasting and Bayesian Data Assimilation" — particle filter and Bayesian DA

### PINNs & ML
- Raissi, Perdikaris & Karniadakis (2019) — "Physics-Informed Neural Networks" (Journal of Computational Physics) — PINN foundational paper

## Regulatory Standards
- **ASME V&V 40-2018** — Verification and Validation in Computational Modeling of Medical Devices
- **ISO 23247** — Digital twin framework for manufacturing
- **FDA Guidance on Computational Modeling and Simulation** (fda.gov)
